# Permit Practice: parent guide

## Put it on her laptop
1. On her laptop, open **Microsoft Edge** and go to https://gotomyrepo.github.io/permit-study/
2. Click the small "App available / Install" icon at the right end of the address bar (or ⋯ menu → Apps → Install this site as an app).
3. Right-click its taskbar icon → Pin to taskbar.
4. Open it once while online and wait a minute, so it can save all the pictures and voice clips. After that it also works without internet.

## How she uses it
- The Home screen shows all 12 lessons as picture tiles, in order: Signs, Yield, Traffic lights, Pavement markings, Right-of-way, Turns, Emergency vehicles, School buses, Speed, Parking, Parking signs, and Alcohol and drugs. A ✓ means that lesson is done.
- Tap the green **▶ Keep going** button to do the next lesson, or tap any tile.
- Every screen talks. 🔊 hears it again. 🔁 **Watch again** plays the picture again. 🏠 goes back home. **▶ Next** turns on once the voice and the picture have finished.
- Each question and its answers are read aloud, with the answer numbers. The small 🔊 on an answer reads just that one. In a lesson, if she picks a wrong answer, the app says "Not quite", replays the picture that teaches it, and lets her try again.
- **📝 Practice test** works like the real one: 20 questions read aloud, 4 of them about road signs. She passes with at least 14 right, including at least 2 of the 4 sign questions (this is the rule on page 10 of the NYS Driver's Manual). Questions come from all 12 lessons, and ones she missed before come up more often. The test doesn't say right or wrong after each answer; at the end it tells her the score, and she can tap **🔁 Practice the ones I missed**.

## 📖 Listen to the manual
This is a spoken retelling of the NYS Driver's Manual, in plain words, with the manual's own pictures. The words light up as they are spoken. Right now it covers Chapter 4 (signs, traffic lights, pavement markings and traffic officers), about 15 minutes of listening. Nothing in it is a test, and she can't get anything wrong.

- **How she starts it:** the **📖 Listen to the manual** button on Home, under the lesson tiles. It picks up where she stopped last time. In the Signs, Yield, Traffic lights and Pavement markings lessons, the **📖 Learn more** button (at the top of the lesson screens and on the "lesson done" screen) opens the matching part of the manual. Her lesson place is saved, so she can come back to the lesson later.
- **The buttons:** along the bottom, ⏮ goes back to the start of the part she is on (tap it twice to go to the part before), ⏸ pauses and ▶ plays again, and ⏭ skips to the next part. At the top right, ☰ shows the list of sections so she can jump to one. 🏠 goes home and remembers her place.
- **It plays on by itself**, one part after another, until the end of the chapter.
- **Internet:** the parts she has already listened to are saved and play without internet. Parts she hasn't heard yet need internet the first time. Without it, the words still show with a note "Can't play this part right now"; she can tap ⏭ to move on, or ▶ to try again once she is back online.

## Check the facts yourself
Open https://gotomyrepo.github.io/permit-study/review.html. The app doesn't link to this page, so bookmark it. It lists every lesson sentence and every question, with the right answer marked, next to the manual's exact words. Each row has a "Manual page" link that opens the manual at that page, and ▶ Listen buttons so you can hear what she hears.

- The manual links need internet (the manual isn't saved in the app).
- If a line is marked ⚠ "Picture in the manual", that fact comes from a picture rather than words, so please look at the picture. (Right now there are none.)
- The practice-test rule is worth checking too: open manual page 10 and look for "at least 14 of the 20 questions".

### The Manual reader tab
At the top of the review page, click **Manual reader**. It lists every part of the retelling (what she hears, with ▶ Listen) beside the manual's own words for that part and a "Manual page" link. Please read and listen to all of Chapter 4 there before she uses it. For each part, check that:
- everything it says is in the manual passage next to it, and nothing important from the passage is missing (rules keep their "unless" and "except" parts);
- it doesn't change the meaning when it puts things in simpler words.

Two spots to look at closely:
- **ch04-markings-12** (the diamond lanes): the manual says a vehicle must meet "the occupancy or other requirements". The retelling says "the rules shown on the signs, such as how many people must be riding in it". Check that this reads right to you.
- **ch04-signs-12** (route signs): press ▶ Listen and check that the voice says "U.S." clearly (as "U S", not as the word "us").

**If something is wrong:** write down the part's id (the small grey label under each retelling, like `ch04-markings-12`), what is wrong, and what the manual says. Send that to whoever updates the app. After it is fixed and published, the corrected part is downloaded again automatically.

## Updates
When a new version is published, it downloads in the background, but it only starts once **every** app window is closed. After an update, fully close the app (all windows, including any Edge tab with the site open) and open it again.

## Progress
Progress is saved on that laptop only. Clearing Edge's browsing data (cookies and site data) resets it.
